# Discovery setup

Start with [API keys and discovery](guide/API-keys-and-discovery.md) for your own private provider keys, outgoing data and provider limitations. Use [Settings and everyday use](guide/Settings-and-everyday-use.md) for ZIP/radii, permissions, schedules and Save/Dismiss.

Version 6 exports preserve provenance and discovery decisions. See [recovery](guide/Backups-and-upgrades.md). Technical behavior is covered by the local tests in `tests/test_discovery.py` and `tests/test_providers.py`; no provider is a guarantee of suitability or complete coverage.
