Public lookups, only with your permission

Zippopotam.us receives your ZIP to find its approximate center; ZIP coordinates are cached locally for 30 days. It also resolves the event venue’s public ZIP. The City of Stanwood receives a request for its public community calendar when within range. Both services can see this server’s public IP. Your ideas, saved plans and household information are never sent. These services may be unavailable.

Optional restaurants, places and ticketed events

Geoapify receives your ZIP, its approximate center, radius and place categories. Ticketmaster receives that approximate center as a geohash, radius and a 90-day date window. Each receives its server-side API key and sees the server’s public IP. No ideas or saved plans are sent. Keys are set by the server owner, never in this page. Each provider requires its own permission below; adding a key alone does not enable lookups.

Provider classifications do not guarantee suitability. Ticketmaster inbox details are cleared after 24 hours; Save keeps your chosen idea as a dated snapshot. Always check its original listing for changes or cancellation.

Weekly refresh starts off. After downtime, one missed run is caught up. Save settings to apply changes or revoke permission; previous suggestions remain. Changing ZIP affects new refreshes, not saved ideas or inbox history.

Category maximums per day

Maximums, not a checklist. Zero excludes a category on that day. Suggestions and relaxation share one weekend time allowance; there are no separate daily time limits.

Randomly reserve up to one relaxation block per day (30 or 60 minutes), only when it fits. These use weekend time, cost zero, and do not use category quotas or the idea count.

Estimates for unknown details

Blank details mean unknown, not free or effortless. Adjust these estimates to fit your household. They only fill blanks during planning; saved plans keep the estimates they used.

Available time and budget cover the whole weekend. Energy is a maximum per idea. Include travel and preparation in durations when they matter. Event dates are checked; clock-time conflicts and travel routes are not calculated.

Data & privacy

Your ideas, plans, ZIP, preferences and discovery decisions are stored on this app’s server for planning and recovery. The server may log request paths and connecting IP addresses. JSON exports and database backups are downloaded only when requested. No analytics, ads, tracking cookies or embedded third-party content are used. Optional discovery sends only the location/search data and provider key disclosed in Discover to the providers you allow; your ideas and plans are never sent. Ticketmaster inbox facts expire after 24 hours; saved ideas and plans remain your snapshots. Linked source sites have their own privacy practices when you open them. Anyone with access to this home-network app can view its data.
